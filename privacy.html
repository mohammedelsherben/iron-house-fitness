Privacy Policy

This page explains what happens to your information when you use the IRON HOUSE FITNESS website.

This is a template for a demo website and not legal advice. Before launching a real gym website, replace it with a policy reviewed by a lawyer.

Who we are

IRON HOUSE FITNESS is a fictional demo gym created for a web development portfolio. No real business, members or payments exist behind this site.

Information the forms handle

The membership, booking, class and contact forms ask for your name and mobile number, plus the plan, trainer, class or message you choose. When you submit, these details are placed in a WhatsApp message that opens on your device. It reaches the gym only when you press Send. We do not store the details on a server. A copy of your latest enquiries is kept in your own browser.

Browser storage and cookies

The site stores your language choice, whether you closed the notices, and your recent enquiries in your browser's local storage. It uses no tracking or advertising cookies.

Third-party services

To display the site, your browser loads photos from Unsplash. If you press Show map, the map is loaded from OpenStreetMap. When you continue on WhatsApp or press the WhatsApp button, you leave this site and WhatsApp's own privacy policy applies. These services may receive your IP address when they load.

Voice features

The Listen buttons use your browser's built-in text-to-speech. The text is read aloud on your own device.

Health information

Programs and exercise guides are general fitness information, not medical advice. The site does not collect health data.

Your choices

You can delete all demo data at any time with Reset demo data in the dashboard, or by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

If this were a real gym

A real gym website must say who is responsible for the data, why each piece of data is collected, how long it is kept, who receives it, and how members can ask to see, correct or delete it. Complete these points with a lawyer before launch.
