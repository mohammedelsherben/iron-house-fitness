Frequently asked questions

Got questions? We have answers

Quick answers about memberships, classes and training.

How do I join IRON HOUSE FITNESS?

Pick a membership plan in the Membership section and fill in the short form. The gym team then contacts you to complete your registration.

Can I try the gym before I commit?

Yes. Book a free intro consultation with a trainer and ask about a trial visit.

Are the programs suitable for beginners?

Yes. Every program shows its difficulty level. Start with Beginner Strength or Functional Fitness, and ask a coach if you are unsure.

Do I need to book classes in advance?

Booking is recommended for busy evening classes. You can also book a one-to-one session with a trainer using the Book trainer buttons.

What are the opening hours?

Saturday to Thursday 06:00 to 23:00, and Friday 10:00 to 20:00.

Can I freeze or cancel my membership?

Annual members can freeze their membership for up to 14 days. Ask the gym team about other options.

Is the exercise guidance medical advice?

No. Programs and exercise guides are general fitness information. Please consult a doctor before starting a new routine, and stop if anything hurts.

What should I bring?

Comfortable training clothes, indoor shoes, a towel and a water bottle. Lockers and showers are available.
